TUMULT

A platform fighter for 1–4 players

Player 1

A / D: move · W: jump
S: block · S + W: drop down
F: attack (press 3× for a combo)
Q · E · R: extra attacks

Player 2

← / →: move · ↑: jump
↓: block · ↓ + ↑: drop down
K: attack (press 3× for a combo)
U · I · O: extra attacks

Good to know

Play vs CPU: you pick your fighter and the CPU's. 2 vs 2: players 3 and 4 use controllers. Empty spots are played by the CPU. Online, up to 4 people can join with the code. Esc returns to this menu.
